Privacy

macro-ai-digest · Effective October 23, 2026

macro-ai-digest is a personal tool, run by its owner for their own use. It isn't offered to anyone else.

Google data

It connects to its owner's own Gmail account with read-only access, so it can't send, change or delete mail. It reads one label of newsletters to build a daily briefing. It processes the messages with AI that runs on the owner's own computer, and keeps the results in private storage. The briefing goes only to the owner's own phone, through a private Telegram bot.

It doesn't share, sell or transfer this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The tool's access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
