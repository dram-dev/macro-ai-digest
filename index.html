macro-ai-digest

A personal tool that builds a daily briefing on markets and AI for its owner: the Fed and markets, China, AI research and the business around it.

It gathers public sources (news feeds, Reddit, Hacker News, SEC and Federal Reserve data, market prices) and one Gmail label of newsletters from its owner's own account. It summarises them with AI that runs on the owner's own computer, and nothing is offered to anyone else.
